## Mapping conventions

When the sheet becomes available, record each historical label or value with its original text, then classify the mapping to a rubric practice or criterion (tier rules are in [rubric.yaml](rubric.yaml)):

- **direct**: same subject and meaning, supported by the sheet's own wording or notes.
- **approximate**: overlapping but not equivalent; state the difference.
- **unsupported**: no sufficient evidence to map; leave unmapped.

Rules:

- Preserve original labels and values verbatim. Historical labels never redefine the provisional tiers.
- If the historical scale is structurally equivalent to the provisional tiers, say so and cite the evidence without building a complicated mapping. Structural equivalence does not prove equivalent assessment criteria.
- Assign no historical tier without sufficient evidence. A historical "yes" does not establish a fully evidenced current tier.
- Compare using the difference categories in [method.md](method.md).
